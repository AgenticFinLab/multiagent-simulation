# Lebanese Civil War Scenario Definition

## 1. Model overview

This dataset-conditioned Rule scenario represents H2EPR-0892 as a sparse
public-record and information-routing process. Eight organizational Agent or
representation-gate parents produce 33 bounded records across 21 logical
coordinates. All 12 source IDs, 40 appearances and 43 Draft action rows remain
accounted for; four passive/unknown sources stay explicit context.

The model covers separately attributable coalition participation, foreign
intervention records, camp-conflict records and a qualified Taif/post-war chain.
It is not a combat, civilian-harm, territorial-control, military-logistics,
constitutional, peacebuilding or responsibility model.

## 2. Event boundary and process coverage

The endogenous record begins with three faction participation records after an
exogenous opening-incident boundary and ends with qualified 1990 post-war
records. It preserves the separate choices of the Lebanese Front, LNM, PLO,
joint-government gate, Syrian and Israeli gates, Amal and Hezbollah.

Unknown gunmen and civilian/refugee cohorts are context. Casualties, damage,
displacement, tactics, territory, weapons, aid, military success, legal
responsibility, parliamentary implementation, disarmament and durable peace
remain outside state. `recorded` means present in this dataset-conditioned run,
not independently verified history.

## 3. Dataset basis, exposure, and temporal firewall

Only `event_spec.json`, `frozen_evidence.json` and `draft_epg.json` are admitted.
All 12 frozen records and all four Draft stages, nine episodes, 12 source IDs,
40 appearances, 43 actor-local rows, 18 relationships and seven transactions
were read. No external reconciliation is performed.

| Dataset issue | Executable treatment |
|---|---|
| secondary, truncated and post-war frozen records | source identities stay sealed; later accounts never become earlier observations |
| April versus September 1975 opening conflict | event boundary follows the Draft while the trigger remains qualified context |
| five malformed E2 transactions | no transfer, route or actor authority is created |
| misdirected E4–E7 relationships | actor-local rows and coherent descriptions own capability |
| five civilian/refugee harm rows | explicit context, never victim-authored intents |
| duplicated Litani and exemption wording | consolidated records with all appearances retained |

Taif and post-war result values remain unrecorded until their generated actions;
their field and capability names are visible earlier. Draft stage descriptions,
Reference content and opaque generated IDs are not participant evidence.

This is a declared-event-vocabulary-exposed baseline: all public field names and each actor's complete capability menu are visible from the first coordinate, including event-specific future terms. Generated result values and actual receipt remain distinct from that vocabulary. No historically prefix-clean information claim is made.

World feasibility is enforced by mechanism domains and state preconditions. Only explicit handler information_requirements are mandatory receipt admission. Other message guards, activation windows and priorities are selected Rule policy assumptions, not backend-neutral historical or institutional laws.

## 6. World, institutions, relationships, and resources

The reducer owns 33 one-time string fields across initial conflict, territorial
records, state authority, Syrian/Israeli intervention, PLO operations, Lebanese
Front records, the War of the Camps, Taif and post-war entities. Every field has
one eligible actor and handler. `unrecorded` is a valid information-dependent
endpoint.

There is no casualty, displacement, territory, force, weapon, ammunition,
finance, aid, siege, supply, vote, institution-composition, legal-status or
peace-effectiveness resource. Messages are qualified reports and requests; they
grant no authority or physical effect.

## 7. Observation and communication routing

Each active actor observes sealed public prestate, current delivered messages,
its own outgoing lifecycle and runtime-derived received/own-action memory.
Another actor's pending item stays private until delivery.

Routes carry faction records, the intervention request, deployment and
alignment notices, PLO/Israeli intervention records, camp notices, mediation,
three Taif positions and post-war notices. Guards use actual delivery where one
participant reacts to another; public state supports only its declared writer's
own accepted sequence.

## 8. Intent, adjudication, lifecycle, and result

Thirty-three event intents plus `no_op` project from eight parents. Rule selects
a typed intent; the environment checks actor, target, parameter and precondition
against one sealed prestate and owns the record delta. Messages have separate
admitted and delivered dispositions.

A request does not deploy forces. Participation does not win a battle. An
intervention record does not control territory. Facilitation does not write the
Front operation. Camp records do not create civilian harm. Mediation does not
form agreement; positions do not implement Taif; enforcement does not measure
peace or constitutional change.

## 9. Configuration, variants, termination, and identity

Shared configuration owns eight actors, 21 coordinates, 33 opening fields,
routes, observations and full-horizon termination. Rule configuration owns
windows, actual-information guards, priorities, qualified payloads and explicit
`no_op`. Every top-level setting has provenance and none is fitted.

Publication requires a complete trace, tick/run seals, replay to exact terminal
state, trace-complete Generated EPG and zero unresolved transport. Outcome
expectations are descriptive. Semantic change rebuilds descendant identities;
route/timing changes require admitted configuration successors.

## 10. Worked cases, falsification, and limitations

- Missing mediation notice: Amal's Taif position, Syrian enforcement and
  post-war records remain open while earlier conflict records close.
- Delayed PLO camp-defence notice: Hezbollah's support and the dependent Taif
  chain can expire without invalidating terminal transport.
- Authority: Syria cannot write a faction position; Israel cannot write the
  Lebanese Front camp operation; a victim context cannot emit an action.
- Result boundary: no accepted action changes casualty, territory, weapon,
  finance, responsibility, peace or constitutional-effect state.
- Result availability: a Taif or post-war result asserted before its admitted
  transition, or a received report without actual delivery, falsifies the package.
  Earlier vocabulary exposure is declared and excludes prefix-clean claims.

This full-Draft logical-time construction supports dataset-conditioned
engineering, behavior-mechanism inspection and simulation-only description. It
does not support historical fit, calibrated conflict behavior, military or
peace effectiveness, held-out performance, causality, scientific validity or
generality.
