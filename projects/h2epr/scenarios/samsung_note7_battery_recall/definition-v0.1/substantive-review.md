## 3. Findings discovered and resolved

### `SC-0481-R01` — transport process could absorb issuance autonomy

- Severity before revision: `BLOCKING`
- Status: `RESOLVED`

CAAC warning and U.S. emergency-order issuance remain separate Agent choices.
Only after valid issuance do publication, effect, routing, duties,
implementation, enforcement, petition, and observable results enter their
Scenario lifecycles.

### `SC-0481-R02` — known chronology could become phase script

- Severity before revision: `MAJOR`
- Status: `RESOLVED`

Seven causal opportunities and eight phases now use state/event entry
conditions. Dates bound admissibility but do not force signals, investigation,
replacement, recall, production, transport issuance, delivery, compliance, or
closure outcome.

### `SC-0481-R03` — remedy semantics were under-conserved

- Severity before revision: `MAJOR`
- Status: `RESOLVED`

The design separately owns offer, eligibility, stock, selection, allocation,
handoff, payment, exchange/refund, custody, failure, and completion. Inventory
and device custody require typed, versioned deltas and conservation checks.

### `SC-0481-R04` — public action could imply universal receipt or effect

- Severity before revision: `MAJOR`
- Status: `RESOLVED`

Authorship, issue, publication, legal effect, route transport, recipient
delivery, acknowledgement, interpretation, implementation, enforcement, and
effect are distinct. Public posting creates no participant observation without
an admitted projection.

### `SC-0481-R05` — heterogeneous Population units could acquire collective state

- Severity before revision: `MAJOR`
- Status: `RESOLVED`

Regional, outlet, consumer, and operator instances are actor-local and scope-
qualified. Population composition and weight are configuration fields; reuse
creates neither collective policy nor shared memory.

### `SC-0481-R06` — future diagnosis and later results could leak backward

- Severity before revision: `MAJOR`
- Status: `RESOLVED`

The observation rules reject January 2017 diagnosis, future recall expansion,
future production or transport action, future enforcement, another actor's
private state, and protected evaluation facts from 2016 decisions.

### `SC-0481-R07` — structural alternatives lacked immutable identity

- Severity before revision: `MINOR`
- Status: `RESOLVED`

Six structural families now require configuration-pinned selections and exact
materializations. A selection is system-only and immutable for a run unless a
separately admitted overlay replaces it.
