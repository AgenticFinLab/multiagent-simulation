### Observations and later work

1. Semantic closure is not executable readiness. A small purpose
   configuration must still choose exact units, initial qualitative states,
   structural variants, exogenous-input bounds, and one conformance lineage.
2. The full nine-product surface should not become the first runtime slice.
   One cross-hop lineage is sufficient to test the highest-risk information,
   authority, delivery, lifecycle, and result boundaries.
3. Government response and collective notification authorization remain
   explicit institutional inputs. A later research question about their
   internal strategic choices would require roster reconsideration, not a
   hidden Scenario policy.
4. The construction is outcome-exposed and uncalibrated. Later scientific
   evaluation, if ever authorized, must state an appropriate non-independent
   or independently reconstructed design; this review makes no such claim.
