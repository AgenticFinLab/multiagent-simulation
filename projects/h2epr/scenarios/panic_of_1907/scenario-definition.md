# Panic of 1907 Scenario Definition

## 1. Model overview

This dataset-conditioned Rule scenario represents H2EPR-0288 as a sparse
institutional decision and public-record process. Eight named or representation-
gate Agents and three Population Models produce 23 bounded records across 20
logical coordinates. Five other source participants remain explicit context;
the absent P_8 sequence value is recorded as an input gap.

The model traces information-sensitive scheme disclosure, aggregate withdrawals,
aid requests and dispositions, trust suspension, liquidity interventions,
convertibility suspension, a qualified gold-flow account and post-crisis reform.
It is not a balance-sheet, market-price, payment, voting or macroeconomic model.

## 2. Event boundary and process coverage

The acute process begins with two separately attributable records of the failed
United Copper scheme and covers member-bank runs, NYCH support, Knickerbocker's
request/denials/suspension, trust-company contagion, private and clearing-house
support, member-bank convertibility suspension and a qualified European gold-flow
record. A coarse later segment represents commission mandate, inquiry,
recommendations and the 1913 Federal Reserve Act record.

United Copper's market price, institution-level solvency and reserves, dollar
flows, withdrawal amounts, the clearing agent, broker behavior, exchange-rate
formation, system recovery and later Federal Reserve operations are outside
state. `recorded` identifies a modeled public or organizational account; it does
not certify the underlying historical proposition.

## 6. World, institutions, relationships, and resources

The reducer owns 23 one-time string fields under scheme, withdrawals,
clearing-house, Knickerbocker, trust-liquidity, private-rescue, containment,
gold-flow and reform entities. Every transition has one eligible actor and one
handler. `unrecorded` is a legitimate endpoint when information is absent.

There are no accounts, cash, securities, loans, gold balances, prices, rates,
bank financials, votes, legal clauses or welfare measures. Message payloads
carry qualified reports, requests, dispositions or directions; they are neither
transfers nor permission to write the recipient's state.

## 7. Observation and communication routing

Each active participant observes sealed public prestate, messages delivered at
the current coordinate, its own outgoing lifecycle and runtime-derived received/
own-action memory. It cannot inspect another actor's pending transport or infer a
receipt from a source-stage label.

Routes carry separately attributable scheme reports, run and assurance notices,
aid requests/denials, suspension and trust-run notices, call-loan stress,
certificate support, convertibility coordination, a public premium signal,
commission mandate and recommendations. Guards require actual delivery. Route
latency is a disclosed structural assumption rather than recovered history.

## 8. Intent, adjudication, lifecycle, and result

Twenty-three event intents plus `no_op` project from eleven parents. The Rule
backend selects an intent; the environment validates actor, target, payload and
preconditions against one sealed prestate. Accepted intent alone changes state.
Coupled messages have independent admitted/delivered/rejected lifecycle.

Scheme participation does not cause a run. A request does not grant aid; a
denial does not prove insolvency. Certificates do not themselves suspend
convertibility. Aggregate gold inflow does not set recovery. Commission work
does not enact legislation, and statute passage does not model central-bank
operation or policy success.

## 9. Configuration, variants, termination, and identity

Shared configuration owns eleven actors, 20 coordinates, opening state, routes,
observations and full-horizon termination. Rule configuration owns windows,
state/message guards, priorities, qualified payloads and explicit `no_op`. Every
top-level setting has exact provenance; none is fitted.

Publication requires the full horizon, hash-chained trace, tick/run seals,
authoritative replay, exact final state, trace-complete Generated EPG and zero
unresolved transport. Descriptive expectations report observed closure and do
not gate release. Semantic change rebuilds all descendant identities; timing or
route changes require admitted configuration successors.

## 10. Worked cases, falsification, and limitations

- Missing aid request: the two aid dispositions and Knickerbocker suspension
  remain open; suspension-dependent trust contagion and containment may remain
  open while earlier records still provide valid execution evidence.
- Delayed recommendations: the Commission can publish, but Congress may receive
  the record after its decision window, leaving the Act record open in a valid
  terminal run.
- Authority: depositors cannot deny aid, NYCH cannot suspend a member bank for
  it, Morgan cannot import gold, and the Commission cannot enact legislation.
- Result boundary: an accepted support record moves no money; a gold-flow record
  creates no recovery; statute passage proves no policy effectiveness.
- Temporal firewall: a 1913 recommendation or institution visible to a 1907
  participant falsifies the package even if final endpoint labels later match.

It does not support historical fit, parameter calibration, held-out performance,
counterfactual rescue or policy effects, causality, scientific validity or
universal generality.
