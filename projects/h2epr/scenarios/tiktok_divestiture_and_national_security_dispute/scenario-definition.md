# TikTok Divestiture and National Security Dispute Scenario Definition

## 1. Model overview

This dataset-conditioned Rule scenario represents H2EPR-0170 as a sparse public-
record and information-routing process. Nine Agent or representation-gate
parents and one Population Model produce 23 bounded records across 22 logical
coordinates. All ten source IDs and 26 Draft appearances remain explicit.

The model covers executive action, legal challenge, review and device
restrictions, public oversight, legislation, judicial records, a temporary
platform-operation record and qualified negotiation positions. It is not a
national-security, constitutional-law, data-flow, speech, market or platform-
impact model.

## 2. Event boundary and process coverage

The endogenous record begins with the August 2020 executive order and ends with
the Draft's September 2025 implementation-grace record. It preserves the
separate choices of Trump, ByteDance, the represented courts, Biden, an
aggregate restriction population, Chew, the House committee, Congress and two
negotiation delegations.

Actual divestment, enforcement, user access beyond one qualified suspension
record, data custody, algorithm control, speech, national-security evidence,
legal reasoning, votes, bilateral implementation and later durability remain
outside state. A `recorded` value means the dataset-conditioned simulation
contains that participant/public record, not that its proposition is verified.

## 3. Dataset basis, exposure, and temporal firewall

Only `event_spec.json`, `frozen_evidence.json` and `draft_epg.json` are admitted.

## 6. World, institutions, relationships, and resources

The reducer owns 23 one-time string fields across regulation, litigation,
judiciary, restrictions, oversight, legislation, platform and negotiation
entities. Every field has one eligible actor and handler. `unrecorded` is a
legitimate information-dependent endpoint.

There is no legal-status engine, ownership ledger, valuation, payment, data or
algorithm resource, content system, user population, vote, compliance measure
or enforcement mechanism. Messages are qualified records or notices and do not
grant another participant's authority.

## 7. Observation and communication routing

Each actor observes one sealed public prestate, current deliveries, its own
outgoing lifecycle and runtime-derived received/own-action memory. Recipients do
not observe private pending messages before delivery.

Routes carry the initial mandate, party filings, court notices, review and
restriction records, hearing invitations/designation/testimony, bill/signature,
appeal/ruling, service suspension, grace, proposal, two negotiation positions
and final notices. Guards use actual delivery; state alone does not impersonate
a private filing or invitation.

## 8. Intent, adjudication, lifecycle, and result

Twenty-three event intents plus `no_op` project from ten parents. Rule selects a
typed intent; the environment checks actor, target, parameter and precondition
against one sealed prestate and owns the delta. Messages have separate admitted
and delivered dispositions.

An order does not divest. A filing does not win. Hearing convening does not
create testimony. Passage does not sign itself. A ruling does not suspend the
platform. A service record does not measure impact. One negotiation position
does not form or implement an agreement.

## 9. Configuration, variants, termination, and identity

Shared configuration owns ten actors, 22 coordinates, 23 opening fields,
routes, observations and full-horizon termination. Rule configuration owns
windows, actual-information guards, priorities, qualified payloads and explicit
`no_op`. Every top-level setting has provenance and none is fitted.

Publication requires complete trace, tick/run seals, replay to exact terminal
state, trace-complete Generated EPG and zero unresolved transport. Outcome
expectations are descriptive. Any semantic change rebuilds dependent identities;
route or timing changes require admitted configuration successors.

## 10. Worked cases, falsification, and limitations

- Missing testimony: PAFACA passage and its dependent law, ruling, service and
  negotiation chain remain open while earlier records still close normally.
- Delayed appeal: ByteDance may file, but delivery after the Supreme window
  leaves that ruling and all dependent choices open in a valid release.
- Authority: ByteDance cannot issue a ruling; Congress cannot sign a bill;
  either delegation cannot write the other's position.
- Result boundary: a passed bill is not enacted law; one delegation position is
  not agreement; a resolution record changes no ownership or algorithm state.
- Temporal firewall: a 2025 resolution term visible to a 2020–2024 actor
  falsifies the package regardless of terminal labels.

This full-Draft logical-time construction supports dataset-conditioned
engineering, behavior-mechanism inspection and simulation-only description. It
does not support historical fit, calibration, national-security or speech
effects, held-out performance, causality, scientific validity or generality.
