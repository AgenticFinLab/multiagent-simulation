The Draft exposes eligibility and an explicit refund-or-replacement option rather than observed uptake. The Population therefore records a constructed request choice and labels it as such; it does not claim that all 1,858 owners acted.

risk score or backend timing parameter. It owns represented meaning and authority;
Rule configuration remains a separate replaceable owner.

## 9. Worked cases and contract falsification

- Normal: After notice delivery, the Population records one aggregate request and sends it to Samsung for information only.
- Missing information: Without actual delivery of Samsung's limited-recall notice, the selected request row waits; eligibility is not treated as received information.
- Pending: Outgoing content is unknown to a recipient until transport admits delivery. The sender sees only its own pending lifecycle.
- Authority/adverse case: An accepted request does not settle a refund. P_5 cannot issue the recall notice or update manufacturer compliance.
- Perturbation: Suppressing the notice leaves request status unrecorded while the limited-recall announcement itself remains valid.

A premature choice, foreign-actor write, future-information leak or undeclared
environment effect falsifies this contract and must fail review or admission.
