## 2. Population scope and representation

P_3's two names describe the same global purchaser cohort before and after recall eligibility. The Population preserves aggregate reporting without inventing individual devices or unanimous behavior.

Recall eligibility is not population-wide return, refund, exchange, injury or verified incident prevalence. The parent fixes no calibrated utility, personality,
risk score or backend timing parameter. It owns represented meaning and authority;
Rule configuration remains a separate replaceable owner.

The two Draft group names remain one cohort. Mainland regular purchasers and test-unit owners have separate parents; their records are not pooled into a global reporting rate.

Aggregation is categorical: an accepted intent records one bounded group-level
choice. No weights, shares, representative individual, or unanimity are inferred.
An independently attributable unit with its own observations or veto requires
roster and semantic review; a purely passive group belongs in scenario/context.

## 4. Event role and relationships

This population may record the represented early incident reports and communicate them to Samsung. It cannot act as another producer,
recipient, regulator, institution or environment process. A message reports a
statement or request; it never transfers the sender's state authority.

The cohort cannot issue a Samsung statement or change a recall field. Its report may be received without forcing a particular manufacturer response.

## 9. Worked cases and falsification

- Normal: The aggregate report is accepted once, sent to Samsung and retained in Samsung's received-memory after delivery.
- Missing information: If the report is absent or delayed, guarded Samsung response rows wait; the environment does not infer a report from the Draft stage label.
- Pending: Outgoing content is unknown to a recipient until transport admits delivery. The sender sees only its own pending lifecycle.
- Authority/adverse case: The cohort cannot issue a Samsung statement or change a recall field. Its report may be received without forcing a particular manufacturer response.
- Perturbation: Withholding this report leaves the early response chain open while later independent institutional rows can still be tested separately.

- Aggregate alternatives: Sales state not yet recorded blocks the selected reporting row; later reporting records only that reports occurred, not the number or severity of incidents.
- Aggregation change: Subdivision by device, country or exposure would need an admitted sampling frame. Replicating the aggregate actor must not multiply incident counts or confidence.
- Rejected request: a stale or unauthorized intent produces no aggregate state
  delta and no accepted-source message; a later retry requires changed visible
  information, not an invented additional population member.

A foreign-actor write, premature generated result or undeclared environment
effect fails this contract. Rule-only windows and receipt guards constrain the
selected policy; mandatory shared prerequisites require an explicit handler
projection. Event-specific capability names are vocabulary-exposed, as declared
in the Scenario, and do not establish historically prefix-clean observation.
