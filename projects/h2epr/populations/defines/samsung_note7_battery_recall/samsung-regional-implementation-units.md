# Samsung regional Note7 implementation units

## 1. Model overview

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Model name | Samsung regional remedy, partner, and safety-communication units |
| Event and interval | Samsung Galaxy Note7 Battery Recall Crisis; 2 September--15 October 2016 |
| Choice unit | One evidence-supported jurisdictional Samsung unit able to coordinate partners or select bounded local remedy and communication intents |
| Population scope | Regional sales, service, and communications interfaces; units are instantiated only where the Scenario supplies event-specific authority and evidence |
| Primary decision situations | Receipt of central direction; local remedy design or revision; stock or partner constraint; renewed stop instruction |
| Aggregation boundary | Units may be summarized by jurisdiction, but authority, information, inventory observation, intent, and result remain unit-local |
| State authority | Scenario owns corporate and legal state, routing, inventory, eligibility, handoff, and remedy completion; units retain local assessments and observed intent lifecycles |
| Evidence use and explanatory scope | Official global, U.S., and Singapore records support a bounded qualitative representation, not a claim that every region had the same discretion |

The model preserves jurisdiction-local coordination choices without turning
Samsung's international organization into either one shared mind or a set of
invented regional executives. The Singapore records anchor an observed unit;
other units require their own Scenario evidence and authority assignment.

## 2. Population scope and representation

One unit is the smallest regional interface that receives bounded corporate
or authority direction and can choose how to coordinate named partners,
communicate local terms, or propose a remedy within its jurisdiction. It is
not a country, brand, newsroom, or employee personality. Several capabilities
may belong to one real Samsung office, but the Scenario must not duplicate
that office's authority, inventory observation, or active commitments across
multiple units.

The population has no default census or weight. An observed Samsung Singapore
interface may be instantiated because official pages document local partner
coordination and exchange/refund arrangements. The existence of that unit
does not prove autonomy in another market. Central product-safety and
production choices remain with the Samsung crisis Agent; carrier and retailer
choices remain with their own population.

A unit returns to Scenario ownership if accepted evidence shows that its
timing, content, channel, and partner actions were fixed implementation with
no local alternative. A named Agent is warranted only if a particular
regional office has unique, causally necessary authority or durable
information that cannot be expressed by the typed unit.

## 3. Evidence and theoretical foundation

The [participant-evidence record](../../../events/samsung_note7_battery_recall/participant-evidence-v0.1.md)
provides source identities, temporal limits, and withdrawal consequences.
This model relies principally on `0481-P-C03`--`0481-P-C04` and the shared
separation in `0481-P-C16`.

Samsung Singapore stated on 11 October that it was working with telecom and
retail partners on a local resolution and exposed a stock-conditioned courtesy
device; on 14 October it announced jurisdiction-local exchange and refund
arrangements across purchase channels. These are direct public actions of the
regional interface. They do not disclose internal delegation, prove partner
receipt, or establish inventory, consumer uptake, or completed remedies.

Two bounded mechanisms remain alternatives: local adaptation to jurisdiction
and partner conditions, and faithful implementation of a central product
posture with only operational choice. Stock, message timing, partner response,
and route availability can distinguish them. No universal regional-autonomy
theory or numerical distribution is adopted.

## 4. Event role and relationships

| Relationship | Unit-owned choice | Other owner |
|---|---|---|
| unit ↔ Samsung crisis interface | acknowledge, question, or locally scope delivered corporate direction | the central Agent owns its direction; Scenario owns delivery and authority matching |
| unit ↔ carrier or retailer | request coordination, communicate terms, or revise a partner-facing offer | each outlet owns its response; Scenario owns routing, stock, and implementation |
| unit ↔ consumer | issue local safety or remedy communication | the consumer owns interpretation and remedy intent; Scenario owns delivery and eligibility |
| unit ↔ local authority | seek clarification or adapt an offer within delivered jurisdictional constraints | the authority owns legal action; Scenario owns legal state and admissibility |

No relation transfers another participant's private assessment. A regional
unit cannot declare global product state, formal recall, partner compliance,
or remedy completion.

## 5. Decision situations, information, and state

| Observation | Unit-specific meaning | Availability and missing behavior | Behavioral use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `delivered_central_direction` | A bounded product-flow, partner, replacement, or safety intent from the central interface | Available only after addressed delivery; publication elsewhere is not receipt | all situations |
| `local_authority_record` | Delivered jurisdiction-specific recall, safety, consumer, or commercial constraint | Scope and effective time remain explicit; missing law is not inferred | local offer and communication |
| `partner_response` | A named carrier or retailer reply, question, refusal, or lifecycle notice | Other partners do not inherit it | coordination and revision |
| `local_inventory_observation` | A dated, route-bounded observation of device or remedy stock | May be stale or incomplete and is not authoritative global inventory | remedy proposal and revision |
| `intent_result_notice` | Acknowledgement, partial result, failure, expiry, cancellation, supersession, or completion of a prior unit intent | Silence keeps the intent unresolved | duplication and reopening |

Each unit retains `local_resolution_assessment`, `open_partner_questions`,
`active_offer_reference`, and `active_intent_references`. They begin unknown
or empty and update only after lawful observations, the unit's own intent, or
a delivered result. They never become copies of legal, inventory, delivery,
or completion truth. Units cannot use another region's private state, a future
recall or transport action, or the January 2017 diagnosis.

## 6. Behavioral model

### Delivered central posture

After receiving an in-scope direction, a unit checks jurisdiction, current
partner routes, and observed local availability. It may request clarification,
propose local partner coordination, communicate bounded safety advice, or
propose a local remedy. A named missing authority or stock fact may support a
finite deferral; it cannot support indefinite silence after a material safety
direction.

### Local remedy under constraints

When the corporate posture permits a remedy but local availability or partner
terms remain unresolved, the unit may propose exchange, refund, courtesy
device, or phased communication terms; seek partner or authority information;
or narrow an offer to what is supported. A proposal cannot state that stock,
eligibility, delivery, or completion already exists.

### Renewed stop or adverse result

A delivered stop direction, partner refusal, expired offer, or materially new
device scope reopens the local resolution. A pending equivalent intent
normally suppresses duplication. Failure, expiry, cancellation,
supersession, or a changed central posture permits revision. The baseline is
set-valued: local adaptation and constrained implementation remain competing
accounts rather than fixed personality types.

## 7. Intent and result boundary

| Intent | Meaning and target | Required content | Scenario-owned result |
|---|---|---|---|
| `request_regional_clarification` | Seek bounded corporate, authority, stock, or partner information | question, jurisdiction, product scope, source, due condition, uncertainty | delivery, access, answer, delay, or failure |
| `coordinate_local_partner_response` | Ask named carriers or retailers to prepare or change a local action | target, action, product class, timing, jurisdiction, review condition | receipt, outlet choice, implementation, inventory effect |
| `propose_local_remedy` | State jurisdiction-local exchange, refund, or courtesy-device terms | eligibility proposal, channel, timing, stock qualification, uncertainty | admissibility, availability, selection, handoff, completion |
| `publish_local_safety_message` | Issue bounded safety or remedy communication through a local route | audience, product identity, advice, terms, issue time | publication, delivery, comprehension, response, effect |

Aggregate counts of unit intents are analysis outputs only. They do not create
a Samsung regional actor or establish uniform implementation.

## 8. Operationalization and uncertainty

The Scenario instantiates only supported units and assigns each a jurisdiction,
authorized channels, partner relations, event-time availability, and lawful
observations. Qualitative dimensions are `central_direction_only`,
`partner_coordination_open`, `local_terms_open`, and `resolution_reopened`.
They are states of a unit's assessment, not historical labels or calibrated
probabilities.

Structural uncertainty concerns whether a local choice existed at all;
compositional uncertainty concerns which units should be instantiated;
measurement uncertainty concerns stock and partner reports. Sensitivity is
expressed by varying delivered authority, partner response, and observed
availability, never by inventing a global regional-discretion rate.

## 9. Worked cases and falsification

**Singapore coordination, reconstructed and exposed.** A unit receives the
global stop posture, observes local partner and stock constraints, and may
coordinate a bounded resolution or request missing terms. Removing the local
partner relation removes that coordination target but does not alter the
central Samsung intent.

**Local exchange/refund announcement, reconstructed and exposed.** The unit
may propose the documented choice set while home delivery, cheque exchange,
stock, and completion remain external. Replacing sufficient local authority
with an unresolved legal constraint changes the response to clarification or
qualified communication.

**Unchanged pending offer, illustrative.** An equivalent unresolved offer is
not reissued merely because time advances. A delivered stock failure or
superseding central stop reopens the choice. The model fails if every region
is instantiated from the Singapore evidence, if a local message creates
inventory, or if publication guarantees consumer receipt.

## 10. Limitations and references

The model does not reconstruct Samsung's regional governance, estimate the
number or composition of units, prove local autonomy outside evidenced
instances, quantify inventory or uptake, or support policy-effectiveness,
calibration, prediction, or cross-jurisdictional generality claims.

References: Samsung Global and U.S. Newsroom statements cited in the shared
participant evidence; Samsung Electronics Singapore, “Update on Samsung
Galaxy Note7,” 11 October 2016, and “Update for Galaxy Note7 customers in
Singapore,” 14 October 2016.
