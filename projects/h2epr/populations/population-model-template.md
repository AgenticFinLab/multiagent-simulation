# Population Model template

Keep the ten modules in order. Use a population only when the choice unit and
aggregation boundary can be stated explicitly.

## 2. Population scope and representation

Define inclusion, exclusion, unit of choice, aggregation, weighting, and the
condition for promoting a unit to a named Agent or returning behavior to the
scenario.

## 3. Dataset basis and provenance

Link source records to the population role and distinguish observed group
labels from executable assumptions about heterogeneity.

## 4. Event role and relationships

Describe resources, claims, institutions, counterparties, and routes. State
which relations are unit-level and which are aggregate world state.

## 5. Decision situations, observations, and state

List activation, observable information, forbidden information, persistent
unit state, aggregate state, and update authority.

## 6. Choice model and heterogeneity

Define admissible responses, minimum response classes, heterogeneity
dimensions, correlation assumptions, adaptation, abstention, and remaining
backend choice. Do not select exact distribution parameters here.

## 7. Intent and environment-result boundary

List unit or aggregate intents, targets, payloads, lifecycle, and the outcomes
that remain environment-owned.

## 8. Configuration and uncertainty

Declare parameter domains, supported instantiation modes, aggregation losses,
sensitivity dimensions, and the owner of exact selected values.

## 9. Worked cases and falsification

Exercise at least two heterogeneous responses, missing information,
aggregation changes, and one environment rejection or partial result.

## 10. Limitations and source anchors

State missing microdata, unsupported heterogeneity, structural alternatives,
and successor conditions.
