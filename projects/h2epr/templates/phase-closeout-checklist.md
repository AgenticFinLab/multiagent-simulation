# Phase closeout checklist

Use this checklist at a maintained boundary, not after every file.

- Inputs are exact, permitted, hash-stable, and free of protected exposure.
- Every added asset has a current consumer and one authority.
- Dataset statements, executable assumptions, generated observations, and
  interpretation remain distinct.
- Roster, Definition/Model, interface, scenario, configuration, realization,
  runtime, and report do not rewrite one another.
- Exact identities, checksums, links, schemas, focused negatives, and affected
  regression tests pass.
- Configuration receipts are independently rederived; implementation sources
  match their pinned bytes; Generated EPG covers every trace record; transport
  has no unresolved terminal state.
- The reading traverses every trace record, graph node, and edge and classifies
  terminal values as closed, persistent, or deliberately open.
- Deterministic A/B compares all scientific outputs and the run receipt;
  identity perturbation is independently derived and hash-linked.
- Accepted parents are immutable; corrections use successor or erratum.
- Current READMEs point to the accepted endpoint rather than a build diary.
- The result states what it establishes and what remains unsupported.

Use one disposition: `complete`, `complete with recorded limitations`, `return
to owning layer`, `owner decision required`, or `incomplete`.
