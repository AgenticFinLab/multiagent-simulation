models. It extends the accepted Frame only where decision ownership,

## Shared behavior and interface findings

The eight models preserve one sender-to-result pattern:

```text
participant-owned choice or message intent
  -> authority, jurisdiction, content, target, and event time
  -> scenario-owned publication, routing, delivery, admissibility, or failure
  -> recipient-local observation and interpretation
  -> recipient-owned next intent
  -> scenario- or authority-owned implementation and result
```

Persistent memory is limited to a participant's qualitative assessment, open
questions, and referenced intent lifecycles. No model receives another
participant's private state, a public statement merely because it exists, the
expanded recall before 13 October, the order before issuance and effect, or the
January 2017 diagnosis during 2016.

## Evidence closure

The seventeen official sources support sixteen participant claims, eight
representations, their decision-time information boundaries, and every
non-participant disposition. Evidence remains bounded for internal Samsung
and CAAC governance, cross-region autonomy, incident-level truth, consumer
uptake, outlet-level compliance, and supplier autonomy. Those uncertainties
are represented as aggregation or instantiation limits, alternative
mechanisms, Scenario state, or reopening triggers rather than invented facts
or numerical probabilities.
