# Substantive review rubric

Use this rubric to test the Agent Definition's scientific content. Valid
headings, filled tables, citations, and long prose are necessary presentation
checks; they do not answer the questions below.

## Scope and claim calibration

- Is the event, interval, participant, and decision scope precise?
- Does the Definition state what it explains and what it leaves to the
  scenario, other participants, and evaluation?
- Are claims limited to what the current event and evidence support?
- Does the document avoid turning a two-role pilot into cross-event validity?
- Is the evidence-exposure status accurate?

## Evidence relevance and integrity

- Does every material historical claim resolve to an adopted source?
- Is the source appropriate for the proposition and time?
- Are source statement, inference, interpretation, and assumption separated?
- Are important source dependencies and contradictions visible?
- Are claim uses no broader than the evidence supports?
- Would withdrawing a claim have a known modeling consequence?

## Theoretical and empirical foundation

- Are original theoretical or empirical works read and cited?
- Is the mechanism attributed to them accurately?
- Are population, period, institution, and transfer limits discussed?
- Does event evidence establish applicability, or is applicability labeled an
  assumption?
- Are competing mechanisms considered?
- Does the theory explain behavior rather than decorate the Definition?

## Participant representation

- Is the historical entity distinguished from the modeled decision interface?
- Are included and excluded people, offices, committees, members, and
  processes clear?
- Is the aggregation rationale connected to the research question?
- Are suppressed internal conflicts and information differences acknowledged?
- Are there evidence-based split triggers?
- Would a coarser representation lose a necessary prediction?
- Would a finer representation add testable explanatory value?

## Institutional fidelity

- Are mandate, jurisdiction, membership, governance, authority, duties,
  procedure, resource control, relationships, and discretion modeled?
- Are formal rules distinguished from observed practice?
- Are owning, controlling, coordinating, requesting, and observing resources
  distinguished?
- Do institutional constraints precede generic goals or preference scores?
- Are modern institutional analogies kept within their transfer limits?

## Epistemic integrity

- Is authoritative world state separate from participant observation?
- Is every observation historically available through a plausible channel?
- Are time, freshness, granularity, uncertainty, and missing behavior clear?
- Are forbidden future and hidden facts explicit?
- Do participant-available observations actually feed the stated mechanisms?
- Is the participant's knowledge no richer merely because the researcher has a
  later archival source?

## Private state and belief

- Is every persistent state behaviorally necessary?
- Are initialization and legitimate update events specified?
- Is persistent memory distinguished from ephemeral reasoning?
- Are beliefs derived only from participant-available observations?
- Is precision appropriate to the historical evidence?
- Could two implementations preserve the same state semantics?

## Behavioral mechanisms

- Does each mechanism have a clear causal process and boundary condition?
- Does it add explanatory content beyond the known outcome?
- Are structural alternatives preserved with their shared fixed boundary,
  conservative baseline, sensitivity role, and retirement evidence explicit?
- Has absence of evidence been kept distinct from evidence of prohibition?
- Can it predict information seeking, delay, abstention, communication, or
  intent under a changed situation?
- Can it fail?
- Is any mechanism merely a generic personality label or code conditional?

## Decision Commitments

- Are focal situations behaviorally informative rather than redundant?
- Are activation, information, state, authority, alternatives, precedence,
  fallback, intent, prediction, and falsifier present?
- Are hard backend obligations separated from scientific hypotheses?
- Does each commitment permit only evidence-justified restriction of behavior?
- Are multiple institutionally permitted alternatives preserved when evidence does not choose one?
- Does every activated commitment have a minimum response and bounded reason
  for abstention?
- Is any strong rule fitted to an exposed action labeled as an event-specific
  calibration hypothesis rather than an independent prediction?

## Intent and environment authority

- Are domain intents historically and institutionally meaningful?
- Are target, prerequisites, lifecycle, quantities, and cancellation clear?
- Is intent separated from delivery, admissibility, execution, and result?
- Can the participant issue an unauthorized or self-realized outcome?
- Are institutional actions distorted into an inherited finance order
  vocabulary?

## Parameters and formalization

- Does every parameter have a behavioral meaning and consumer?
- Are unit, domain, range or ordering, source, status, and uncertainty clear?
- Are unidentified quantities represented honestly?
- Are structural uncertainties kept outside a numerical confidence score?
- Are equations and examples dimensionally, logically, and arithmetically
  correct?
- Would a qualitative or procedural representation be more faithful?

## Worked cases and falsifiability

- Do cases cover normal and boundary behavior?
- Are observed, reconstructed, illustrative, and counterfactual cases labeled?
- Do cases obey the same information and authority constraints?
- Is the environment still responsible for results?
- Do perturbations distinguish roles or mechanisms?
- Are there concrete forbidden patterns and revision triggers?
- Was exposed evidence incorrectly presented as independent validation?

## Scholarly quality and provenance

- Can a domain reader understand and critique the model without code?
- Is historical and technical terminology defined consistently?
- Do citations support nearby claims?
- Is prose analytical rather than promotional or compliance-driven?
- Are tables informative rather than a substitute for reasoning?
- Are limitations candid and consequential?
- Does version provenance explain substantive model changes?
- Are implementation and project-management details kept out of the public
  Definition?
