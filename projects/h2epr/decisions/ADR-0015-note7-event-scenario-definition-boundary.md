# ADR-0015: Note7 Event Scenario Definition boundary

- Date: 31 August 2026
- Status: accepted for authoring-window candidate release
- Event: `H2EPR-0481`

## Context

The fixed mapping requires world ownership for product safety signals,
information delivery, investigation, product and remedy state, recall and
transport authority, physical and institutional results, and twelve shared
lifecycles. The Scenario must close those meanings without scripting the known
outcome.

## Decisions

### `OD-0481-SC-01` — causal and temporal boundary

Use an event-driven 19 August--15 October 2016 model with seven causal
opportunity families and eight process phases. Calendar bounds constrain
eligibility but do not guarantee transitions.

### `OD-0481-SC-02` — process ownership

Keep device/incident, supplier-facing investigation, product and production,
inventory/remedy/custody, recall, route/delivery, and post-issuance transport
work as Scenario or institutional processes. They may adjudicate participant
intents but may not impersonate a participant choice.

### `OD-0481-SC-03` — structural baseline

Use six bounded structural families: exogenous hazard-signal pressure, route
and delivery, population assembly, authority capacity, operational result,
and public-action delivery. Configuration must pin one member and its exact
materialization for each family.

### `OD-0481-SC-04` — interface and claim closure

Close all 8 products, 40 observations, 28 private-state placements, 37
intents, and twelve lifecycle families. Preserve the 2017 future-information
firewall and the engineering-only claim boundary.

## Consequences

Scenario Configuration may select exact actors, units, records, variants,
inputs, policies, sensitivities, completion settings, and one bounded lineage.
No selection may preload a known outcome. Independent final review remains
with the original max supervisor.
