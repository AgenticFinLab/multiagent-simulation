# ADR-0005: accept the Panic of 1907 Event Scenario Definition boundary

- Status: accepted
- Date: 22 August 2026
- Scope: H2EPR-0288 Event Scenario Definition v0.1
- Resolved decisions: OD-SC-01 through OD-SC-04

## Context

Roster Definition release v0.1 fixes seven Agent Definitions and five
population models. The accepted consolidated mapping shows that Contracts V1
can carry their semantic interfaces, but an event-level authority is still
needed for time, world state, institutions, relationships, resources,
information delivery, shared business processes, adjudication, results,
structural variants, and termination.

The reviewed Scenario Definition closes all 12 released products, 115
observation placements, 107 intent placements, 13 lifecycle families, and 34
cross-object rules. No released interface lacks a scenario producer, route,
authority, lifecycle, adjudicator, or result owner, and no concrete Contracts
V1 counterexample was found.

## Decision

The accepted analytic boundary begins on 18 October 1907. The primary acute
window is 21–26 October, while the exact early-November terminal date and time
must be selected and justified by a versioned scenario configuration. Event
time is partially ordered; the known chronology constrains opportunities and
information but does not script the outcome.

Pre-boundary affiliated-bank distress, constitution of the trust-company
presidents' committee by the wider presidents' forum, Treasury public
deposits, later clearing-house certificate-facility supply and rules, and NYSE
governance are exogenous scenario inputs. Their autonomous decision makers are
outside Roster release v0.1, so the environment may deliver their dated effects
without inventing hidden Agents or explanations.

The v0.1 structural baseline uses:

- `NO_EVIDENCED_COMPETENT_ALTERNATIVE_ROUTE` for the NYCH support route;
- `NO_NYCH_DIRECTION_DELIVERED` for NBC direction provenance;
- `PROCEDURE_CONSERVATIVE` for the presidents' committee;
- independent participant resource owners;
- named personal coordination as the Morgan attribution baseline; and
- a disabled relationship-history mechanism.

Evidence-bounded alternatives remain unvalidated structural sensitivities.
The selected interpretation must enter scenario and run identity and is never
an Agent observation.

The Event Scenario Definition and release-wide interface closure are accepted
as semantic design authorities. Exact actor composition, population units and
weights, opening resources and obligations, venue and service policies, and
timing parameters are deferred to a separate versioned configuration. Frozen
Agent Definitions and Contracts are reopened only after a reproducible failure
is routed to their owning layer.

## Consequences

Scenario Definition v0.1 is the sole event-world semantic authority for the
next configuration cycle. The earlier semantic skeleton remains a provenance
input and Roster-production precursor rather than a competing full scenario.

The next eligible design task is a minimal scenario configuration that pins
the exact horizon, actor and population assembly, initial projections,
structural choices, routes, and executable policy references. This decision
does not authorize policy implementation, simulation, Rule v2, Contracts
changes, LLM/RAG, G5, historical calibration, or a scientific-validity claim.
