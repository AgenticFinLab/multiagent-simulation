# ADR-0008: accept the SingHealth Event Scenario Definition boundary

- Status: accepted
- Date: 25 August 2026
- Scope: H2EPR-0616 Event Scenario Definition v0.1
- Resolved decisions: OD-SC-05 through OD-SC-08

## Context

Roster Definition release v0.1 fixes nine participant products and six
non-participant dispositions. The accepted consolidated mapping shows that
Contracts V1 can preserve their interfaces, but an event-level authority is
still required for time, technical and institutional state, information
delivery, shared lifecycles, adjudication, results, structural variants, and
termination.

The reviewed Scenario closes all nine products, 62 observation placements, 44
private-state placements, 54 intent placements, and eleven shared lifecycle
families. No released interface lacks a source, route, time rule, authority,
lifecycle, adjudicator, or result owner.

## Decision

The accepted analytic interval begins around 23 August 2017 and runs through
the 20 July 2018 public announcement, with patient-notification delivery
observed through 23 July. Event-specific participant response begins on
18 January and the acute response window begins on 11 June. Calendar dates
bound admissible opportunities and information; they do not script phase
transitions or historical success.

External attack attempts are a bounded adversarial input process. Endpoint and
account context, office availability, distinct MOH/MCI/CSA responses, and
collective notification authorization and delivery remain explicit exogenous
or institutional processes. They may alter authoritative state or produce
routed observations but may not become hidden Agent policies.

The structural baseline uses a bounded adversarial opportunity process,
institution-preserving technical and operational responsibility units,
capacity-qualified office routes, and distinct institutional recipient
histories. Attack pressure, route and delivery, unit composition, office
capacity, technical-result policy, and notification process remain separately
pinned structural alternatives.

Event Scenario Definition v0.1 and its complete interface closure are accepted
as semantic design authorities. Exact units, assignments, qualitative initial
states, structural selections, exogenous sequences, policies, and the first
bounded lineage belong to a separate Scenario Configuration and later
admission stages.

## Consequences

Scenario Definition v0.1 is the event-world semantic authority for the next
H2EPR-0616 configuration cycle. The research roster and semantic skeleton
remain release inputs and participant-production precursors rather than
competing full Scenario authorities.

The next eligible task is a small, non-executable purpose configuration. It
must pin the declared units, initial projections, structural choices,
exogenous-input bounds, and one high-information lineage without implementing
the full roster.

This decision does not authorize a loader, executable binding, participant
policy, runtime, simulation, calibration, evaluation, Contracts change, or
historical/scientific-validity claim.
