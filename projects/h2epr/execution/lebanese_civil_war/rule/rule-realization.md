# Lebanese Civil War Rule realization review

## Decision ownership

The realization maps eight exact actor IDs and 33 event intents plus `no_op` to
the maintained declarative Rule engine. Rule owns selection; semantic parents
own meaning, the environment owns admission/effects, MASim owns transport and
trace order, and publication independently derives evidence.

## Behavior and temporal review

Rows use inclusive windows, sealed public state and actual delivered-message
memory. Coalition, intervention, camp, mediation, faction-position, enforcement
and post-war records remain separately owned. One actor emits at most one action
per coordinate.

## Failure and reproducibility review

Missing information produces waiting/open endpoints. Foreign intents or targets
are typed rejections with no delta. Implementation sources are exact-hashed;
model/network fallback is absent. Fresh A/B, generated-ID perturbation, replay,
graph, seals and independent rematerialization remain release obligations.

## Boundary

Acceptance demonstrates deterministic attachment to one admitted package. It
does not validate history, battlefield behavior, casualty or responsibility
claims, foreign-intervention effects, Taif implementation, peace, causality or
scientific generality.
