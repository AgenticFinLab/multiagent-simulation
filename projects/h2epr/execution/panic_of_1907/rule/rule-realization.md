# Panic of 1907 Rule realization review

## Decision ownership

The realization maps eleven exact actor IDs and 23 event intents plus `no_op` to
the maintained declarative Rule engine. Rule owns row selection only. The
backend-neutral package owns semantics, the environment owns admission/effects,
MASim owns transport/trace order, and publication independently derives evidence.

## Behavior and temporal review

Rows use inclusive windows, public prestate and actual delivered-message memory.
Joint-scheme records remain separately attributable. Requests, denials, support,
suspension, gold-flow, commission work and legislation remain separable. One
actor emits at most one action per coordinate; `no_op` is explicit waiting.

## Failure and reproducibility review

Missing information produces valid waiting/open endpoints rather than Draft-
stage autocompletion. Foreign intents or targets are typed rejections with no
delta. Implementation sources are exact-hashed; model/network fallback is absent.
Fresh A/B, generated-ID perturbation, replay, graph, seals and independent
rematerialization remain run-release obligations.

## Boundary

Acceptance demonstrates deterministic attachment to one admitted semantic
package. It does not validate timing, balances, actor preferences, historical
fit, rescue or reform effects, causality or scientific generality.
