# Angola Yellow Fever Outbreak of 2016 shared configuration

## Identity and purpose

Configuration `h2epr.0551.comparison.v2` selects the sole shared-world values for `H2EPR-0551`. It is full-Draft-exposed and supports deterministic engineering and descriptive simulation only.

## Exact inputs

The configuration binds roster `h2epr.0551.roster.v1`, actor map `h2epr.0551.actor-map.v2`, participant interface `h2epr.0551.participant-interface.v2`, Scenario Interface `h2epr.0551.scenario-interface.v2`, and Mechanism `h2epr.0551.scenario-mechanism.v2`. The compiler independently derives its admission receipt from those bytes and the sealed Draft.

## Shared comparison settings

Twenty coordinates cover all nine Draft episodes plus a delivery barrier. Eight active actors, 26 opening fields, 17 directed routes, sealed observations, mandatory coordinate completion, and zero unresolved transport are explicit. The two passive source groups remain world-state dispositions. Every top-level setting has one provenance pointer and no exemption.

## Validation and promotion

Promotion requires Draft roster equality, semantic-parent anchors, actor/intent/state/route closure, independently rederived receipt equality, fresh package compilation, runtime replay, and publication verification. Any selected value change requires a new configuration identity and rerun.

## Behavior and finite-horizon choices

All routes use one logical tick. Paired reports can arrive separately and remain
known; Population guidance received at c13 remains usable at c14. Rule rows have
bounded waiting windows ending by c19, followed by the c20 delivery barrier.
These intervals are structural, not calibrated latency or epidemiological time.

National `response_recorded`/`fractional_response_recorded` labels and Uganda's
`not_declared`/`declared_ended` labels are institutional records. They do not
measure response effectiveness or infection state. Uganda's declaration does
not require WHO permission. Outcome expectations describe the chosen baseline;
they do not decide whether an actual finite run is valid. Failed integrity or
incomplete transport leaves diagnostic custody rather than a complete release.
