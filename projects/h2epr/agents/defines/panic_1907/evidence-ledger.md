# Panic of 1907 Agent Definition evidence ledger

This ledger is the claim-level authority for the current Knickerbocker Trust, New York Clearing House, and
National Bank of Commerce Definitions. Source identities, public locators, adopted passages, file hashes, and
source-specific limits are recorded in the adjacent [source register](source-register.md).

## Reading rules

- `direct` means the cited source states, records, or measures the proposition for the stated scope.
- `bounded inference` names a model consequence that follows only with the stated qualifications.
- `disputed` preserves incompatible source accounts or mechanisms.
- `unknown` and `unavailable` are deliberate model states, not missing prose to be filled from intuition.
- All focal outcomes and later historical accounts are `FULL_DRAFT_EXPOSED`. They may inform construction,
  worked cases, and falsification design, but not an independent held-out claim.
