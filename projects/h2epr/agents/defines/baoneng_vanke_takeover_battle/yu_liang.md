## 9. Worked cases and contract falsification

- Normal: After the initial stake report is known, Yu can state the management concern to Wang and the corporate interface.
- Missing information: An absent initial stake report prevents the selected statement Rule from firing; an absent later nominee notice leaves his memory without that proposal, not without a fabricated role transition.
- Pending: Its outgoing statement remains unknown to a recipient until delivery. The sender can observe its own pending lifecycle but cannot mark it delivered.
- Authority or adverse result: Neither Wang endorsement nor Metro nomination gives Yu permission to record a board rejection or declare himself elected.
- Perturbation: A delayed initial stake notice delays Yu's statement and Wang's republication through separate received-memory dependencies.

Premature action before the stated information condition contradicts this Rule
realization. A foreign actor writing the record, future nominee knowledge in an
early observation, or a notice generating securities/election effects contradicts
the shared semantic contract and must fail review or admission.
