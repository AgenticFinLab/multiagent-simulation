## 9. Worked cases and contract falsification

- Normal: On receipt of the proposal it may record its own opposition; that record can coexist with Baoneng opposition and Metro participation.
- Missing information: An unseen proposal leaves its position unrecorded under the selected Rule. Silence is not support.
- Pending: An incoming proposal remains unknown until delivery. This realization sends no outgoing message for this actor; its own pending projection stays empty.
- Authority or adverse result: The environment rejects China Resources attempting to write Baoneng opposition or a Vanke board decision.
- Perturbation: A delayed proposal can produce different timing for China Resources without changing another shareholder authority.

Premature action before the stated information condition contradicts this Rule
realization. A foreign actor writing the record, future nominee knowledge in an
early observation, or a notice generating securities/election effects contradicts
the shared semantic contract and must fail review or admission.
