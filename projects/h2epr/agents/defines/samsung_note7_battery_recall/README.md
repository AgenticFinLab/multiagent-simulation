# Samsung Galaxy Note7 Agent Definitions

The collection contains four event-bound authority interfaces:

- [Samsung Note7 crisis decision interface](samsung-note7-crisis-decision-interface.md);
- [U.S. CPSC recall decision interface](cpsc-recall-decision-interface.md);
- [CAAC warning decision interface](caac-warning-decision-interface.md); and
- [U.S. DOT emergency-order decision interface](us-dot-emergency-order-decision-interface.md).

Four adjacent Population Models preserve distributed choices without creating
collective participants:

- [Samsung regional implementation units](../../../populations/defines/samsung_note7_battery_recall/samsung-regional-implementation-units.md);
- [carrier and retail remedy outlets](../../../populations/defines/samsung_note7_battery_recall/carrier-and-retail-remedy-outlets.md);
- [Note7 owners and prospective consumers](../../../populations/defines/samsung_note7_battery_recall/note7-owners-and-prospective-consumers.md); and
- [air-transport operators](../../../populations/defines/samsung_note7_battery_recall/air-transport-operators.md).

The [participant evidence](../../../events/samsung_note7_battery_recall/participant-evidence-v0.1.md)
owns their claims and participant-time limits. The
[participant interface](../../interfaces/samsung_note7_battery_recall/)
connects them to regional, intermediary, consumer, and transport-operator
populations while keeping delivery and results outside participant policy.

The [Roster Definition release v0.1](../../../releases/samsung_note7_battery_recall/roster-definition-v0.1/)
pins the eight models, shared interface, evidence authorities, semantic
skeleton, and all disposition-only rows. The products are qualitative and
event-bound; their exposed cases are explanatory and falsifying surfaces, not
independent validation.
