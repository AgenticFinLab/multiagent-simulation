## 2. Benchmark participant and representation

P_1 is retained as a disclosed representation gate because Whitefly is named only in retrospective March 2019 material. Its structural actor identity supports two attributed process records; it is not evidence that other 2018 actors knew the attribution.

risk score or backend timing parameter. It owns represented meaning and authority;
Rule configuration remains a separate replaceable owner.

This agent may record the dataset's attributed access and concentrated exfiltration actions without exposing technical strategy or early public attribution. It cannot act as another producer,

## 9. Worked cases and contract falsification

- Normal: The gate records a qualified attributed-access entry and then the concentrated-exfiltration account; neither sends early attribution information.
- Missing information: The two attributed records need no incoming runtime message. If either selected row is omitted, no environment default may synthesize it from a stage label.
- Pending: This realization emits no outgoing message for this participant; incoming content remains unknown until actual delivery.
- Authority/adverse case: No SingHealth, IHiS or government actor may impersonate this gate. An accepted attributed-activity record does not reveal Whitefly identity to other actors or force detection.
- Perturbation: Withholding later Symantec publication leaves public attribution open while these retrospective process records remain structurally available.

A foreign-actor write, premature generated result or undeclared environment
effect fails this contract. Rule-only windows and receipt guards constrain the
selected policy; mandatory shared prerequisites require an explicit handler
projection. Event-specific capability names are vocabulary-exposed, as declared
in the Scenario, and do not establish historically prefix-clean observation.
