## 4. Event role, relationships, and authority

This agent may issue the initial divestment-or-restriction order, record the represented defence, and issue separately bounded negotiation and implementation extensions. It cannot act as another producer,
recipient, regulator, institution or environment process. A message reports a

## 9. Worked cases and contract falsification

- Normal: The interface records the 2020 order and defence, later records negotiation grace after the represented service suspension, and records implementation grace only after the qualified resolution notice arrives.
- Missing information: The defence row waits for ByteDance's delivered challenge. The final extension waits for a delivered qualified resolution record; absent information leaves the row open.
- Pending: Outgoing content is unknown to a recipient until transport admits delivery. The sender sees only its own pending lifecycle.
- Authority/adverse case: An order record does not itself divest, ban or transfer anything. A grace extension neither proves agreement nor changes platform operations.
- Perturbation: Delaying the resolution notice can leave the final extension open without changing the earlier order or litigation records.

A premature choice, foreign-actor write, future-information leak or undeclared
environment effect falsifies this contract and must fail review or admission.
