## 9. Worked cases and contract falsification

- Normal: The gate publishes its own qualified position to ByteDance, the US delegation and Trump without writing another party's state.
- Missing information: The gate waits for both the grace notice and ByteDance proposal. If either is absent, its position remains unrecorded.
- Pending: Outgoing content is unknown to a recipient until transport admits delivery. The sender sees only its own pending lifecycle.
- Authority/adverse case: One side's position is not bilateral agreement or implementation and creates no ownership or operational effect.
- Perturbation: Withholding the corporate proposal leaves both delegation positions and the downstream resolution open.

A premature choice, foreign-actor write, future-information leak or undeclared
environment effect falsifies this contract and must fail review or admission.
