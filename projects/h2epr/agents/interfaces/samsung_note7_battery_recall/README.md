# Samsung Galaxy Note7 participant interface

## Purpose and participants

This account connects four authority-bearing Agent interfaces and four
heterogeneous populations without creating shared knowledge or self-realizing
actions. The [participant evidence](../../../events/samsung_note7_battery_recall/participant-evidence-v0.1.md)
owns claim support; the [research roster](../../rosters/samsung_note7_battery_recall.md)
owns membership and dispositions.

## Cross-participant chains

### Product-safety and remedy chain

```text
delivered incident signal
  -> Samsung crisis interface investigates and emits a product-flow,
     replacement, partner, communication, or production intent
  -> Scenario routes bounded direction to regional and intermediary units
  -> each unit observes local authority, stock, partner state, and requests
  -> regional/intermediary intent is adjudicated into local availability,
     communication, exchange, refund, or stop-action state
  -> consumers observe only delivered warnings and local remedy state
  -> individual reporting, use, purchase, exchange, or refund intent
  -> Scenario owns device, handoff, completion, and aggregate result
```

### U.S. consumer-safety authority chain

```text
delivered firm report and incident/remedy record
  -> CPSC interface chooses warning, information/remedy review, formal recall,
     expansion, or bounded deferral
  -> Scenario owns publication and jurisdictional recall state
  -> intermediaries and consumers observe only delivered notice
  -> local implementation or remedy intents
  -> Scenario owns legal, inventory, delivery, completion, and effectiveness results
```

### Transport chains

CAAC and U.S. DOT remain separate issuers. Each Agent consumes only its
jurisdictional safety and recall predicates and may issue, seek information,
qualify, or defer a restriction. After issuance, Scenario owns publication,
effective time, routing, operator obligations, enforcement, petition or review
process, and result. Operators then make only the local communication,
handling, denial, escalation, or stricter-measure choices their delivered
authority permits.

## Interface closure

| Sender | Intent family | Recipient observation | Recipient choice | External result owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Samsung crisis interface | investigation request, product-flow direction, replacement or partner request, safety communication | regional unit, intermediary, CPSC, or consumer receives bounded content after delivery | local implementation, authority review, remedy, or safety response | Scenario owns delivery, inventory, legal status, and effect |
| Regional unit | partner coordination, local remedy offer, local communication | named carrier, retailer, or consumer receives jurisdiction-local terms | outlet implementation or consumer remedy intent | Scenario owns stock, eligibility, handoff, and completion |
| CPSC interface | warning, remedy-review request, recall or expansion intent | public, Samsung, intermediaries, and consumers receive separate routed records | corporate, outlet, or consumer response | Scenario owns publication, recall state, and effectiveness |
| Consumer | incident report, power-down/use, purchase/refrain, exchange/refund request | Samsung, CPSC, or intermediary receives a bounded request or signal | independent investigation, authority, or remedy choice | Scenario owns device state, delivery, admissibility, and completion |
| CAAC interface | warning issuance or information request | operators receive jurisdictional content after routing | communicate, handle, deny, or adopt a stricter measure | Scenario owns warning state, enforcement, and result |
| U.S. DOT interface | emergency-order issuance or information request | operators receive the order subject to publication and effective time | notify, deny, handle, or escalate | Scenario owns legal effect, enforcement, petition, and result |
| Transport operator | passenger/cargo communication, carriage denial, handling, stricter-measure proposal | passenger, shipper, airport, or authority receives local action | independent response where represented | Scenario owns admissibility, physical carriage, enforcement, and effect |

## Information, state, and falsification boundaries

- A public page is not a participant observation until its route and delivery
  are established.
- Participant assessments and open requests are isolated; authoritative recall,
  inventory, device, transport, and result state is never copied into private
  memory.
- An unresolved intent suppresses a duplicate only while its lifecycle remains
  current. Failure, expiry, cancellation, supersession, or material new
  evidence reopens the choice.
- Original devices, replacement devices, and green-icon replacements retain
  distinct identities; a replacement label is not a safety result.
- The interface fails if an intent directly changes authoritative state, if
  one participant reads another's private assessment, if publication implies
  universal receipt, if CAAC and U.S. authority are merged, or if January 2017
  findings enter any 2016 choice.
