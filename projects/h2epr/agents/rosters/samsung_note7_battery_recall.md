# H2EPR-0481 research roster

- Version: `0.1`
- Status: accepted research scope
- Event: Samsung Galaxy Note7 Battery Recall Crisis
- Definition release: [`v0.1` semantic release](../../releases/samsung_note7_battery_recall/roster-definition-v0.1/)

This roster identifies the participants and processes required by the
accepted event question. It is a qualitative research roster, not an
executable actor list: four rows have Agent Definitions, four have Population
Models, and the others remain Scenario or institutional processes, context,
or excluded aftermath.

## Research boundary

H2EPR-0481 asks how incident and defect signals moved through corporate
investigation, replacement handling, intermediary and consumer response,
consumer-safety authority, and transport restriction between 19 August and
15 October 2016. Post-crisis evidence through 23 January 2017 may inform
research interpretation but never participant-time information.

The known outcome informed construction. The roster and its models are
qualitative and uncalibrated; they do not establish historical fit,
prediction, policy or recall effectiveness, defect cause, liability,
scientific validity, or cross-jurisdictional or universal generality.

The roster preserves organizational, jurisdictional, unit, and device
boundaries. It gives no corporation, regulator chain, intermediary cohort,
consumer population, or operator population one mind or shared private state.

## Definition release gate

Roster Definition release v0.1 is complete when its manifest and checksums pin
this roster, the semantic skeleton, both evidence authorities, all eight
participant products, and the shared interface. The release remains a semantic
research input. It contains no Scenario Definition, mapping, configuration,
policy, runtime, simulation, generated graph, calibration, or evaluation.

## Change policy

Roster v0.1 is frozen for the accepted question once released. A new
participant requires evidence that an omitted autonomous choice is causally
necessary. Later phases may map and instantiate the released semantics but may
not silently change a disposition, information boundary, authority, intent,
or result owner. Such a change requires a reviewed successor.
